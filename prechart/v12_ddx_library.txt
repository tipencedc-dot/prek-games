## DIFFERENTIAL LIBRARY (v12)
Format per condition:
[condition] | supports: [history or tests that raise it] | separate with: [best test or
cluster] | rule-out: [finding that, when negative or denied, makes it less likely]
"rule-out" lists only high sensitivity findings (negative result lowers probability).
"rule-in" findings are high specificity (positive result raises probability) and are
named under "separate with". A single negative low sensitivity test (for example
Spurling) never makes a condition "less likely" on its own.

### CERVICAL
Mechanical neck pain / facet | supports: local pain, pain on extension and rotation,
segmental tenderness, no arm signs | separate with: absence of radicular cluster |
rule-out: none
Cervical radiculopathy | supports: dermatomal arm pain or paresthesia, reflex or
myotomal
change | separate with: Wainner cluster (ULTT A, Spurling, distraction, rotation under
60
deg to the involved side; 3 of 4 positive raises probability) | rule-out: ULTT A
negative
Cervical myelopathy | supports: clumsy hands, gait change, bilateral symptoms, UMN signs
|
separate with: Cook cluster (gait deviation, Hoffmann, inverted supinator, Babinski, age
over 45; 3 of 5 raises probability) | rule-out: 0 to 1 of the Cook cluster
Cervicogenic headache | supports: unilateral side-locked headache provoked by neck
movement or posture | separate with: flexion rotation test, upper cervical palpation
reproducing familiar headache | rule-out: flexion rotation test negative (case-control
data only)
Thoracic outlet / brachial plexus | supports: positional arm symptoms, overhead
provocation | separate with: refer if vascular signs | rule-out: none reliable
Shoulder pathology referring to neck | supports: pain with shoulder motion | separate
with: shoulder exam, cervical exam | rule-out: none
RED FLAG: vertebral or carotid artery dissection | screen: 5 Ds / 3 Ns, new severe
headache or neck pain unlike prior | action: SAFETY FLAG
RED FLAG: upper cervical instability | screen: trauma, RA, Down syndrome, Sharp-Purser |
action: SAFETY FLAG

### HEADACHE
Cervicogenic headache | as above
Tension-type headache | supports: bilateral pressing, no aggravation by routine
activity,
pericranial tenderness | separate with: ICHD-3 criteria | rule-out: none
Migraine | supports: pulsating, moderate to severe, aggravated by activity, nausea or
photophobia and phonophobia | separate with: ICHD-3 criteria | rule-out: none
Medication overuse headache | supports: analgesic use 10 to 15 or more days per month |
separate with: medication history | rule-out: none
RED FLAG: secondary headache | screen: SNNOOP10 | action: SAFETY FLAG on any positive

### THORACIC
Mechanical thoracic pain / costovertebral dysfunction | supports: local pain, pain with
rotation or deep breath, rib spring reproduction | separate with: segmental palpation |
rule-out: none
Thoracic radiculopathy / post-herpetic | supports: band-like dermatomal pain, rash
history
| separate with: history and sensation | rule-out: none
Scoliosis | supports: rib hump or asymmetry | separate with: Adams forward bend | rule-
out:
Adams negative
RED FLAG: cardiopulmonary, visceral, or compression fracture | screen: exertional pain,
dyspnea, visceral pattern, osteoporosis or steroid use with trauma | action: SAFETY FLAG

### LUMBAR / SI
Nonspecific mechanical low back pain | supports: pain that varies with posture and
movement, no neuro signs | separate with: absence of radicular and SIJ clusters |
rule-out: none
Lumbar radiculopathy (disc) | supports: leg pain below knee worse than back pain,
dermatomal pattern | separate with: crossed SLR (positive raises probability), slump,
reflex or myotomal change | rule-out: SLR negative
Upper lumbar radiculopathy (L2 to L4) | supports: anterior thigh pain | separate with:
femoral nerve stretch, patellar reflex, quadriceps strength | rule-out: none reliable
Lumbar spinal stenosis | supports: age over 48, bilateral leg symptoms with walking or
standing relieved by sitting or flexion | separate with: no pain when seated, Cook
cluster (bilateral symptoms, leg pain more than back pain, pain with walking or
standing, relief with sitting, age over 48; 4 of 5 raises probability) | rule-out: none
of the 5 Cook items present
SI joint pain | supports: pain below L5 over PSIS, no centralization | separate with:
Laslett cluster (distraction, thigh thrust, compression, sacral thrust, Gaenslen; 3 of 5
raises probability) | rule-out: fewer than 3 of the Laslett tests positive
Facet mediated pain | supports: extension and rotation provoke, no neuro signs |
separate with: none reliable on exam | rule-out: none
Hip pathology referring to low back | supports: groin pain, limited hip IR | separate
with: hip exam | rule-out: full painless hip IR
RED FLAG: cauda equina syndrome | screen: saddle anesthesia, bowel or bladder change,
bilateral or progressive weakness | action: SAFETY FLAG
RED FLAG: fracture, malignancy, infection | screen: trauma, osteoporosis, steroids,
cancer history, weight loss, fever, night pain, IV drug use | action: SAFETY FLAG

### HIP
Hip osteoarthritis | supports: age over 50, groin pain, morning stiffness under 1 hour,
limited IR | separate with: Sutlive cluster (squatting aggravates, lateral pain with
active flexion, scour with adduction painful, active extension painful, IR 25 deg or
less; 4 of 5 raises probability) | rule-out: none reliable alone
FAI / labral | supports: younger, groin pain with flexion and rotation, clicking |
separate with: imaging (FADIR does not rule in) | rule-out: FADIR negative
Gluteal tendinopathy / GTPS | supports: lateral hip pain, side-lying pain, tender
greater
trochanter | separate with: single leg stance 30 s, resisted external derotation |
rule-out: greater trochanter nontender
Lumbar referral | supports: back pain, neuro signs, normal hip exam | separate with:
lumbar exam | rule-out: none
RED FLAG: occult hip fracture, AVN, septic hip | screen: fall with inability to bear
weight, steroid or alcohol history, fever | action: SAFETY FLAG

### SHOULDER
Subacromial pain / RC tendinopathy | supports: lateral arm pain, painful arc | separate
with: Park cluster (Hawkins Kennedy, painful arc, ER weakness; all 3 raises probability)
| rule-out: none reliable alone; all 3 Park items negative lowers probability
Full thickness RC tear | supports: age over 60, weakness, night pain | separate with:
drop
arm, ER lag (positive raises probability) | rule-out: none reliable on exam
Adhesive capsulitis | supports: loss of passive ER, diabetes, age 40 to 60 | separate
with: PROM equal to AROM loss, ER most limited | rule-out: full passive ER
AC joint pathology | supports: superior shoulder pain | separate with: cross body
adduction, AC tenderness | rule-out: AC joint nontender
Glenohumeral instability | supports: younger, dislocation history | separate with:
apprehension, relocation | rule-out: none
Cervical referral | supports: neck pain, dermatomal arm symptoms | separate with:
cervical exam, Spurling | rule-out: none
RED FLAG: cardiac referral, fracture or dislocation, infection, tumor (Pancoast) |
screen:
exertional pain, trauma deformity, fever, mass, apical lung history | action: SAFETY
FLAG

### ELBOW
Lateral elbow tendinopathy | supports: lateral pain with gripping | separate with:
Cozen,
Mill, lateral epicondyle tenderness | rule-out: none reliable (no specificity data)
Medial elbow tendinopathy | supports: medial pain with gripping | separate with: reverse
Cozen, medial epicondyle tenderness | rule-out: none reliable
Cubital tunnel syndrome | supports: ulnar digit paresthesia | separate with: elbow
flexion test with pressure (most sensitive), Tinel | rule-out: flexion plus pressure
negative
Radial tunnel / cervical C6 to C7 referral | supports: lateral forearm pain, neck pain |
separate with: cervical exam | rule-out: none
RED FLAG: fracture, distal biceps rupture, septic bursitis | screen: elbow extension
test
after trauma, hook test, erythema with fever | action: SAFETY FLAG

### WRIST / HAND
Carpal tunnel syndrome | supports: nocturnal median paresthesia, shaking relieves |
separate with: Wainner CTS cluster (shake relief, wrist ratio over 0.67, symptom
severity, diminished thumb sensation, age over 45), carpal compression, Phalen |
rule-out: none reliable on exam
De Quervain tenosynovitis | supports: radial wrist pain with thumb use | separate with:
Finkelstein, first dorsal compartment tenderness | rule-out: none reliable
CMC osteoarthritis | supports: thumb base pain with pinch, age over 50 | separate with:
CMC grind, imaging | rule-out: none
Cervical C6 to C7 referral | supports: neck pain, dermatomal pattern | separate with:
cervical exam | rule-out: none
RED FLAG: scaphoid fracture | screen: fall on outstretched hand with snuffbox
tenderness | action: SAFETY FLAG and imaging

### KNEE
Patellofemoral pain | supports: anterior knee pain with stairs, squatting, sitting |
separate with: pain with squat, exclusion of other structures | rule-out: none reliable
Meniscal lesion | supports: twisting mechanism, locking or catching, effusion | separate
with: McMurray, Thessaly, joint line tenderness | rule-out: none reliable alone
ACL tear | supports: noncontact pivot, pop, rapid effusion | separate with: Lachman
(rule-out when negative), pivot shift (rule-in when positive), lever sign | rule-out:
Lachman negative
PCL tear | supports: dashboard or fall on flexed knee | separate with: posterior drawer
|
rule-out: posterior drawer negative
MCL / LCL sprain | supports: valgus or varus mechanism | separate with: valgus or varus
stress at 30 deg | rule-out: stress test negative
Knee OA | supports: age over 50, morning stiffness under 30 minutes, crepitus, bony
enlargement | separate with: ACR clinical criteria | rule-out: none
Pes anserine bursitis / tendinopathy | supports: medial pain below joint line | separate
with: pes anserine tenderness | rule-out: nontender pes anserine
Hip or lumbar referral | supports: normal knee exam | separate with: hip exam | rule-
out:
none
RED FLAG: fracture, septic joint, DVT | screen: Ottawa knee rules, fever with hot joint,
calf swelling | action: SAFETY FLAG

### ANKLE / FOOT
Lateral ankle sprain (ATFL / CFL) | supports: inversion mechanism, lateral swelling |
separate with: delayed exam at day 4 to 5 (swelling, hematoma, ATFL tenderness, anterior
drawer), talar tilt | rule-out: ATFL nontender, or delayed exam negative
Syndesmotic (high ankle) sprain | supports: external rotation mechanism, pain above
ankle mortise | separate with: squeeze test, ER stress | rule-out: none reliable
Plantar heel pain | supports: first step pain, medial calcaneal tenderness | separate
with: plantar fascia origin tenderness, windlass | rule-out: none reliable
Achilles tendinopathy | supports: pain 2 to 6 cm above insertion, morning stiffness |
separate with: Achilles tenderness, Thompson negative | rule-out: none
Tarsal tunnel syndrome | supports: medial plantar paresthesia | separate with: tarsal
tunnel Tinel | rule-out: none
RED FLAG: fracture, Achilles rupture | screen: Ottawa ankle rules, Thompson | action:
SAFETY FLAG and imaging or referral

### TMJ
TMD myalgia | supports: jaw pain with function, masticatory muscle tenderness | separate
with: DC/TMD familiar pain on palpation | rule-out: none
TMJ arthralgia | supports: pain localized to joint | separate with: familiar pain with
joint palpation or movement | rule-out: none
Disc displacement with reduction | supports: clicking | separate with: reproducible
click on opening and closing | rule-out: none
Disc displacement without reduction | supports: locking history, opening under 40 mm |
separate with: limited opening with deflection | rule-out: opening 40 mm or more
RED FLAG: infection, neoplasm, giant cell arteritis | screen: fever, swelling, new
malocclusion, jaw claudication over age 50 | action: SAFETY FLAG
